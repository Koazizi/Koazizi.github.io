Upcoming

  • 14 Nov 2026

    Departmental seminar at Tennessee State University

    Talk in the Department Seminar Series on coupled human–infrastructure dynamics in urban flood systems.

2026

  • Aug 2026

    New project: roadway flooding and emergency access in Nashville

    A one-year award from the CHDT University Transportation Center supports a coupled hazard–network–behaviour framework for understanding how flooded roads disrupt access to critical services across Nashville. Koorosh Azizi is principal investigator; the project runs to July 2027. Project details.

  • Jul 2026

    Flood-governance networks paper out in Water Resources Research

    “Gaps and opportunities for collaborative flood governance: network-analytic insights from the US Gulf Coast”, with R. Patrick Bixler and Paola Passalacqua. Code and network data are on GitHub. DOI.

  • 2026

    Paper accepted in Nature Cities

    “Coupled human–environmental systems: socio-behavioral and spatial dynamics in green stormwater infrastructure adoption”, with Margaret Garcia, Dev Niyogi and R. Patrick Bixler — an agent-based account of why household adoption of green stormwater infrastructure diverges so sharply across a city.

  • 2026

    UJIMA Faculty Award

    Awarded by Tennessee State University.

  • Feb 2026

    Flood exposure and green infrastructure adoption published

    In Environmental Research Communications, with R. Patrick Bixler: testing how well household adoption of green stormwater infrastructure lines up with actual flood exposure. DOI.

  • Jan 2026

    The lab opens at Tennessee State University

    Koorosh Azizi joined the Department of Civil and Architectural Engineering as Assistant Professor and founded the Urban Hydrosystems & Resilience Lab. The lab is recruiting its first graduate students.
