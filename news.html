Research, awards and talks

Updates from the UHR Lab and the development of Koorosh Azizi's research program.

2026

  • 14 Nov 2026

    Departmental seminar at Tennessee State University

    Upcoming invited talk in the Department Seminar Series on coupled human-infrastructure dynamics in urban water and flood-risk systems.

  • Aug 2026

    Paper accepted in Nature Cities

    “Coupled human–environmental systems: socio-behavioral and spatial dynamics in green stormwater infrastructure adoption,” with Margaret Garcia, Dev Niyogi and R. Patrick Bixler, was accepted in August 2026.

    Publication record · Related research

  • Aug 2026

    New project on roadway flooding and emergency access in Nashville

    A CHDT University Transportation Center award supports a coupled hazard, transportation-network and behavior framework for understanding how flooded roads disrupt access to critical services across Nashville. Koorosh Azizi is principal investigator; the project runs through July 2027.

    Project context

  • Jul 2026

    Flood-governance networks paper published in Water Resources Research

    “Gaps and opportunities for collaborative flood governance: network-analytic insights from the US Gulf Coast,” with R. Patrick Bixler and Paola Passalacqua, examines the actual collaboration networks underlying preparation and response.

    DOI · Code and data

  • 2026

    UJIMA Faculty Award

    Koorosh Azizi received a UJIMA Faculty Award from Tennessee State University.

  • Feb 2026

    Flood exposure and green infrastructure adoption paper published

    The Environmental Research Communications paper tests how household green stormwater infrastructure adoption relates to flood exposure, parcel feasibility and neighborhood conditions.

    DOI · Code and data

  • Jan 2026

    The UHR Lab opens at Tennessee State University

    Koorosh Azizi joined the Department of Civil and Architectural Engineering as an Assistant Professor and founded the Urban Hydrosystems & Resilience Lab.

    Research profile · Student opportunities

2025

  • Dec 2025

    Presented household stormwater-adoption research at AGU25

    The presentation in New Orleans examined socio-behavioral dynamics of household stormwater management through a spatially explicit agent-based model.

  • 2025

    Multi-sectoral flood-governance paper published

    “Integrating perspectives: multi-sectoral insights into U.S. Gulf Coast flood governance” was published in the International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction.

    DOI

  • 4 Mar 2025

    Invited seminar at James Madison University

    School of Integrated Sciences Seminar Series.

  • Jan 2025

    Miami-Dade water-management transition paper published

    “Balancing effectiveness and equity in sustainable water management transitions” was published in the Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management.
