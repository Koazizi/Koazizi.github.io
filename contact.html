Prospective students

Start with Join Us. It sets out what is available, what background helps, and the four things to put in your email. If I have not replied within two weeks, send a reminder — it is not an imposition.

Agencies, utilities & community organisations

If you work on flooding, drainage or stormwater in Middle Tennessee and think the lab could be useful — or you have a dataset that has never been analysed properly — please write. Collaboration of this kind is the point of the work, not a distraction from it.

Media and public enquiries

Email directly for comment on urban flooding, stormwater infrastructure, flood insurance or water affordability.
