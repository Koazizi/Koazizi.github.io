Building the Urban Hydrosystems & Resilience Lab

UHR Lab is a new research group at Tennessee State University. The lab is currently recruiting its first graduate cohort and developing collaborations across water resources, climate resilience, infrastructure and governance.

Principal Investigator

Lab director

Koorosh Azizi, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor · Civil & Architectural Engineering · Tennessee State University

Koorosh is an interdisciplinary water-resources scholar studying the nexus of urban water and environmental systems. His research focuses on climate risk assessment, sociohydrology, coupled human–water–infrastructure systems, human–environment interactions, equity and resilience.

His work combines hydrologic and hydrodynamic modeling with agent-based modeling, system dynamics, GIS and remote sensing, spatial and network analysis, surveys and interviews.
