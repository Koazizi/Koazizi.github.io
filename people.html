Koorosh Azizi, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Civil and Architectural Engineering at Tennessee State University and director of the Urban Hydrosystems & Resilience Lab.

Koorosh AziziAssistant ProfessorCivil & Architectural EngineeringTennessee State University

Principal Investigator

Researching the human and institutional dynamics behind urban water reliability

My research examines how infrastructure, human behavior and institutions interact to shape the reliability and equity of urban water and flood-risk systems.

At the UHR Lab, that question spans urban flooding, water affordability and utility transitions, green stormwater infrastructure, household adaptation and governance networks. The common goal is to understand why technically similar systems produce different outcomes across places and communities, and how analysis can better support decisions.

Before joining Tennessee State University in January 2026, I was a postdoctoral fellow at The University of Texas at Austin, working across the Jackson School of Geosciences and the LBJ School of Public Affairs. Previously, I was a postdoctoral research scholar in the School of Sustainability at Arizona State University, where I studied urban drinking-water transitions. I completed my Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering at The University of Memphis in 2022 as a Herff Graduate Fellow, with community-based research on urban flooding in North Memphis.

What I value in students

Curiosity across disciplinary boundaries, comfort learning quantitative tools, willingness to write and revise carefully, and the ability to connect a model to the people or institutions it represents.

Useful backgrounds include civil and environmental engineering, hydrology, geography and GIS, data science, planning, public policy and environmental social science.
